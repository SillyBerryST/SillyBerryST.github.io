Privacy Policy — Block Rush

Publisher: SillyBerry Studio Applies To: Block Rush (Standalone Build) Last Updated: November 2026
Studio Policy Note: SillyBerry publishes separate, accurate policies for each game based on its real technical design. This Privacy Policy describes Block Rush in its current standalone architecture and will be reviewed and updated before any online services or advertisements are introduced.

1. Overview

SillyBerry (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. Block Rush is built as a standalone block-placement puzzle game. In its current standalone design, Block Rush does not connect to SillyBerry backend servers to collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To make the game work smoothly across play sessions, Block Rush stores gameplay information strictly locally on your device using standard browser storage (such as localStorage) or local application storage. This local data includes:

  • Game progress, high scores, and gameplay statistics.
  • Unlocked achievements and selected visual themes.
  • In-game audio, display, or control preferences.

This information remains on your device under your control. Clearing your browser cache/site data or uninstalling/resetting the app will erase locally saved progress.

3. No Online Accounts, Cloud Saves, or Analytics Backend

In its current standalone version, Block Rush does not include:

  • User registration, login systems, or online player profiles.
  • Cloud save synchronization across devices.
  • SillyBerry-operated databases or third-party analytics tracking backends.

4. Score Sharing & Local Image Downloads

Block Rush allows players to share their game results. Sharing works exclusively through your device’s built-in sharing capabilities or by downloading a generated score image locally to your device. SillyBerry does not intercept, upload, or store copies of shared scores or images.

5. Advertising & Future Releases

The current standalone design described in this policy does not integrate an active advertising SDK or third-party ad network. Advertisements may be added in certain future versions or releases of Block Rush. We do not claim that any specific advertising provider is active prior to actual integration. If advertisements or external third-party services are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated accordingly before those features go live.

6. Website Hosting

Our official studio website (https://sillyberry67.github.io/) is hosted via GitHub Pages. Standard web server technical logs (such as IP address and browser user-agent) may be processed automatically by GitHub Pages as part of delivering static web pages, subject to GitHub’s own privacy practices.
