Privacy Policy

Block Rush · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Release note: This policy describes the current standalone game design. If advertising, analytics, online accounts, cloud saves or other third-party services are added or changed in a release, this page must be updated before that release is distributed.

SillyBerry ("we", "our" or "us") develops Block Rush. This page explains what information the game handles and how it is used.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device using browser or app storage. This can include scores, records, level progression, play statistics, achievements, unlocked items, game sessions, settings, and profile fields such as your player name and bio. In the current standalone design, this information is not sent to or stored on SillyBerry's own game servers.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the browser or app data. Doing so may permanently erase your progress and settings.

2. Sharing features

If you choose to use the score-sharing feature, the game asks your device or browser to open its available sharing interface, or offers a local image download. Information is shared only through the option you choose; SillyBerry does not receive a copy through a game server.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Some planned or platform-specific releases may offer optional rewarded advertisements. If an advertising provider is enabled, that provider may process technical information such as an advertising identifier, IP address, device information or cookies to deliver and measure ads, subject to its own privacy policy. The provider and data practices must be identified in this policy when selected and integrated. Do not assume that ads are active in every version of Block Rush.

4. Analytics and online accounts

The current standalone design described here does not use a SillyBerry-operated account system, cloud-save service or analytics backend. If these services are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the information involved.

5. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services, if enabled in a particular release, may have their own age requirements and privacy practices. Parents or guardians who have a privacy concern can contact us.

6. Security and retention

Locally stored game data remains on the device or browser profile until it is cleared or removed by the user or operating system. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the game or its services change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact SillyBerry at sillyberrystudio@gmail.com.
