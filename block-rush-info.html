Block Rush

A crisp, strategic block-placement puzzle game built by SillyBerry. Plan every move, clear lines across multiple game modes, track personal statistics, unlock achievements, and customize your board themes.

Core Features

Designed around smooth tactile controls, clear progression, and offline-ready local storage.

▦

Block-Placement Strategy

Fit varied block shapes onto the board to complete full horizontal or vertical lines and maintain high-scoring combo streaks.

⚙

Multiple Game Modes

Switch between distinct modes tailored for thoughtful puzzle solving or fast-paced score chasing.

★

Progression & Stats

Track best scores, cleared lines, and session milestones stored directly in your local device or browser storage.

🏆

Achievements

Complete gameplay challenges and skill milestones to unlock built-in achievements as you improve.

🎨

Visual Themes

Personalize the look and feel of the board with selectable themes crafted for comfortable play.

⇪

Local Score Sharing

Share your results using your device's native share sheet or save a score image directly to local storage.

Technical & Data Transparency

Local-First Storage

All player progress, statistics, achievements, themes, and settings are saved locally in browser or application storage on your device.

No Server Accounts

The current standalone design does not use SillyBerry servers, online accounts, cloud saves, or an analytics backend.
